# Portfolio design direction

## North star

A cybersecurity field console translated into an editorial portfolio: precise, technical, and human. The site should feel like a practiced operator's workspace, not a neon “hacker” theme.

## Visual language

- Obsidian (`#05070d`) is the primary ground.
- Cobalt (`#4f7cff`) identifies systems and authored work.
- Signal coral (`#ff5b4d`) marks intervention and emphasis.
- Cyan (`#67e8f9`) identifies telemetry and focus.
- Pale technical paper (`#e9eef9`) separates the project archive from the dark narrative.
- Display: Space Grotesk. Body: Manrope. Data labels: DM Mono.

## Signature

The hero contains a pointer-responsive 3D signal field: a spatial network that represents the relationship between identity, cloud, and agent activity.

## Motion contract

Motion communicates depth and inspection. It is restrained to the signal field, reveal sequence, and shallow project-card tilt. All continuous and spatial motion is disabled when reduced motion is requested.

## Accessibility

WCAG 2.2 AA contrast, visible keyboard focus, semantic links and headings, 16px body baseline, touch-safe controls, and responsive reflow without horizontal scrolling.
